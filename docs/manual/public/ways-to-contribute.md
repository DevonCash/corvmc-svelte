## Supporting CorvMC

The [Contribute](/contribute) page lays out how you can support the collective.
Volunteering comes first, because shows here are run by volunteers.

## Volunteer

The page lists the roles you can sign up for, grouped three ways:

- **At shows** — host, tech (soundboard), door, merch, and photo or video.
- **Away from shows** — street team, tabling at community events, work parties,
  gear repair, and audio engineering.
- **Committees** — Programming, Production, Development, Communications, Art and
  Merchandise, and Facility. Committees meet monthly to guide the organization.

**Sign Up to Volunteer** opens our volunteer interest form in a new tab. Picking
a role isn't a commitment — it tells us who to contact when something comes up,
and we provide whatever training the role needs. The form also has room for
program, club, or class ideas, for showing art or performing, and for "not sure,
I just want to help."

## Become a sustaining member

Monthly contribution on a sliding scale starting at $10/month. Sustaining
membership funds the space and unlocks benefits like free practice hours, a
recurring weekly practice slot, and discounts on tickets and gear. **Explore
Membership** takes you to the [membership](/membership) page, where you'll be
guided to sign in or create an account.

## Other ways to help

- **One-Time Donation** — opens our donation form on Zeffy in a new tab. Zeffy is
  zero-fee for nonprofits, so the full amount reaches CMC.
- **Donate gear** — instruments or equipment for the lending library.
- **Host a workshop** — songwriting, recording, gear repair, theory.

The gear and workshop cards link to the [contact form](contact-and-perform.md)
so we can follow up with you.

## Already a member?

Manage your sustaining membership any time from your member area.
